Bio

Cole Dabney started in the hardcore scene, shooting bands from the pit at Warped Tour before there was a barricade. He's since become a favorite director across genres — from country artists in the hayfields to pop stars in the big city.

That range led him to become Jennifer Lopez's go-to videographer, traveling with her around the world on tours and to award shows, festivals like Coachella, and Netflix film sets. His most recent music video, "Save Me Tonight" from J.Lo and David Guetta, was released through Warner Records.

Cole's debut feature film as producer, COTTON CANDY BUBBLE GUM, premiered at the SXSW Film Festival and was acquired by Shout! Factory for worldwide distribution after winning Best Comedy at the Hollywood Film Festival.
